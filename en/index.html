Good solutions start with understanding the problem. The process is an approach, not a guarantee; it adapts to scope and requirements.

How I work

  1. 01

    Initial Discussion

    Clarify the current situation, goals and challenges.

  2. 02

    Requirements

    Define services, expected deliverables and constraints.

  3. 03

    Concept

    Develop and agree on a suitable approach.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    Build in clear steps and keep decisions transparent.

  5. 05

    Review and Handover

    Evaluate the result against agreed criteria and hand it over.

What you can count on

No performance figures, no promises, just working principles that apply to every project.

  • Direct collaboration

    You talk to the person who does the work. No intermediate layers.

  • Traceable decisions

    Open issues and decisions are documented, not made silently.

  • The right solution, not new software by default

    Not every task needs a new tool. We first check what fits the actual need.

  • Verifiable results

    What is handed over has been checked against the agreed criteria, with sources and documentation.
